YOUR DATA AT A GLANCE
01

Your camera collects data

Video and device information are collected. Audio is included only when enabled.

VideoAudio, if enabledDevice information
Why is this needed?

Video enables live view and recordings. Device information helps connect and maintain the camera. Microphone access should be explained separately when enabled.

02

Your data travels to storage

Recordings follow your device's storage settings. Cloud storage is a separate, optional choice in this prototype.

Camera → Local storage→ Cloud, if enabled
What is sent, and where?

Local recordings stay on the configured camera or HomeBase. A real implementation must show the selected model's verified storage location, transfer method and retention period here. Account and connection services may still process data when video is stored locally.

03

Access has a person and a purpose

You access your camera to view your home. Invited people receive the permissions you give them.

Who can access what, and why?

You: live view and recordings, to monitor your home.
Invited users: only the access you choose, to share monitoring.
Service providers: any processing needed to operate selected services must be named and explained in the full policy. This preview does not verify eufy's actual access practices.

04

You manage recordings and access

Review sharing permissions and storage settings. Deleting your recording may not remove copies someone has already downloaded.

What about deletion and risks?

Retention and deletion timing depend on the device and service. A real agreement must state those periods. Shared or downloaded copies may remain outside your control. Account compromise, unintended sharing and service breaches can expose data; no system can promise zero risk.

About this preview
This is a proposed interface for a university project, not an official eufy agreement. Data flows and permissions require verification against the device and current policy.
